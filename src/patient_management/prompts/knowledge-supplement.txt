TASK
Provide concise Chinese clinical knowledge for physicians tied to THIS patient's documented diagnoses and active problems.
Ask: what medical knowledge would most help understand this patient NOW?
This is not a dashboard tutorial, patient education, disease encyclopedia or another patient summary.

INPUT
Selected patient diagnoses/facts with stable IDs, and selected fresh content-verified external evidence with ext_* IDs.

OUTPUT
One JSON object matching knowledge-supplement.schema.json: {"knowledge_items":[]} or at most 3 items.
Fields: title, why_relevant, knowledge, clinical_connection, evidence_ids, external_refs, uncertainty (low/moderate/high).
No Markdown, HTML, layout or navigation instructions.

RULES
- Prefer the patient's main documented disease or a clinically important current problem.
- Prefer mechanisms, diagnostic traps, clinical-test discordance, complications, red flags,
  treatment monitoring, follow-up principles and relevant guideline knowledge supported by the supplied evidence.
- Choose special or complex features with practical clinical value; avoid repeating overview or assessment.
- Separate general medical knowledge from the patient's actual findings and explain their bounded connection.
- No item merely because a lab is abnormal. Abstain if nothing adds genuine value.
- Uncertainty grades the patient-specific connection, not source tier; association never proves causation.
- Keep general disease knowledge distinct from conclusions about this patient.
- A candidate diagnosis stays a possibility; never describe it as confirmed.
- Every item cites patient evidence_ids for relevance and external_refs for medical knowledge.
- Use only the supplied verified medical evidence. Search snippets, stale sources and model memory are not evidence.
- No invented criteria, numbers, examination results, drug doses or orders.
- Do not fill the section with chart-reading advice, UI instructions or generic health tips.
- If no high-value, supported content is available, return {"knowledge_items":[]}.
- Content generation requires a separate explicit host step; opening or refreshing the dashboard never generates it.
